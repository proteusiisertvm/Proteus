WELCOME TO RHETOR 6.0

On behalf of Proteus, the Biology Club of the Indian Institute of Science Education and Research (IISER) Thiruvananthapuram, we would like to extend our heartfelt invitation to you for Rhetor 6.0, our annual student-organised symposium. This year's symposium will be held at our picturesque campus in Thiruvananthapuram, Kerala. It is scheduled for the 23rd, 24th, 30th and 31st of August.

About Rhetor

Rhetor is a national-level science symposium organized by IISER Thiruvananthapuram, featuring talks by eminent scientists, interactive workshops, and vibrant discussions. The event aims to foster curiosity, collaboration, and scientific thinking among students and researchers. This year's Rhetor's theme is “Codes of creation: Deciphering life's blueprints”. We aim to invite researchers from the different disciplines of biology, to shed light on Life's Blueprints and how modern science works to decipher this code that creates all living beings We believe that your work and interests align perfectly with this year's theme.

Workshops & Activities

Participate in hands-on workshops, panel discussions, and networking sessions. Rhetor 6.0 offers a unique platform to learn, share, and connect with peers and experts from across the country.
