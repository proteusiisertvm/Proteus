23rd August | 11:45 AM
This study uncovers a non-neuronal role of the Qa-SNARE protein STX1A in HeLa cells. We found STX1A localizes mainly to lysosomes, where its overexpression reduced lysosome number and dispersion, while depletion increased peripheral lysosomes but impaired proteolytic activity and exocytosis. Mechanistically, STX1A pairs with SNAP23 and VAMP2 to drive lysosomal exocytosis. These results reveal a novel role for STX1A in lysosomal dynamics, essential for membrane and organelle repair, extending SNARE function beyond neurons.

23rd August | 2:30 PM
Loss of Heterozygosity (LOH) from mitotic recombination is a major yet underexplored source of genetic variation. Using hybrid Saccharomyces cerevisiae and whole-genome sequencing, we show LOH is widespread and contributes more to genomic change than base mutations. We generated an LOH hotspot map, discovered the regulator gene IRC20, and found that pre-existing LOH promotes further instability, similar to cancer cells. In industrial yeast, LOH drives colony morphology changes that impair bioethanol fermentation. These findings highlight LOH as a central mechanism shaping genome evolution and phenotypic diversity.

This workshop explores the emerging role of long non-coding RNAs (lncRNAs) as key regulators of gene expression. Once dismissed as transcriptional noise, lncRNAs are now recognized for their diverse regulatory roles—acting as decoys, scaffolds, guides, signaling molecules, and molecular sponges.
